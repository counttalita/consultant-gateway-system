## Getting Started

### What is the Consultant Gateway?

The Consultant Gateway is your central hub for managing your relationship with Up Time Consulting. Through this portal, you can:

- Complete your onboarding
- Update your profile and skills
- Manage your availability
- View your project assignments
- Update your banking details

### System Requirements

- Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- Email access for OTP codes
- Internet connection

---

## Logging In

### First Time Login

1. Navigate to the portal URL provided by Up Time Consulting
2. Enter your email address
3. Click "Request OTP Code"
4. Check your email for a 6-digit code
5. Enter the code within 10 minutes
6. You're logged in!

### Subsequent Logins

The process is the same every time - no passwords to remember!

1. Enter your email
2. Request OTP code
3. Check email
4. Enter code
5. Access portal

### Troubleshooting Login

**"I didn't receive the OTP email"**
- Check your spam/junk folder
- Wait a minute and try again
- Ensure you entered the correct email address
- Contact support if issue persists

**"My OTP code expired"**
- OTP codes expire after 10 minutes
- Request a new code
- Enter it promptly

**"I'm being rate limited"**
- You can request up to 3 OTP codes per 15 minutes
- Wait 15 minutes and try again

## Onboarding

### Overview

When you first log in, you'll be guided through a 5-step onboarding process:

1. Personal Information
2. Banking Details
3. Skills & Experience
4. Contract Signing
5. Welcome

### Step 1: Personal Information

**What you'll need:**
- Full legal name
- South African ID number
- Contact phone number

**Tips:**
- Use your legal name as it appears on official documents
- Double-check your ID number for accuracy
- Provide a mobile number where you can be reached

### Step 2: Banking Details

**What you'll need:**
- Bank name
- Account number
- Branch code
- Account type (Cheque/Savings)

**Important:**
- Your banking details are encrypted and secure
- This information is used for monthly payments
- Ensure accuracy to avoid payment delays

**Supported Banks:**
- FNB, Standard Bank, Nedbank, Absa, Capitec, and others

### Step 3: Skills & Experience

**Option A: Upload Your CV**
1. Click "Upload CV"
2. Select your CV file (PDF or DOCX)
3. Wait for parsing to complete
4. Review extracted skills
5. Add or remove skills as needed
6. Click "Confirm"

**Option B: Manual Entry**
1. Click "Enter Manually"
2. Type your skills (one per line or comma-separated)
3. Add experience details
4. Add qualifications
5. Click "Save"

**Tips:**
- Be specific with skills (e.g., "Change Management" not just "Management")
- Include certifications and qualifications
- Update regularly as you gain new skills

### Step 4: Contract Signing

1. Review the consulting agreement
2. Scroll to the bottom
3. Provide your digital signature
4. Click "Accept and Sign"

**Note:** You can download a copy of the signed agreement for your records.

### Step 5: Welcome

Congratulations! Your onboarding is complete.

**What happens next:**
- Your profile is activated in our system
- You're added to Harvest for time tracking
- You're set up in Xero for payments
- You'll receive a welcome email with next steps

## Managing Your Profile

### Accessing Your Profile

1. Log in to the portal
2. Click "Profile" in the navigation menu
3. View or edit your information

### Updating Your Bio

1. Navigate to your profile
2. Click "Edit Bio"
3. Write a brief professional summary (max 1000 characters)
4. Click "Save"

**Tips for a good bio:**
- Highlight your expertise and experience
- Mention key achievements
- Keep it professional and concise
- Update when you complete major projects

### Updating Skills

1. Navigate to your profile
2. Scroll to "Skills" section
3. Click "Edit Skills"
4. Add new skills or remove outdated ones
5. Click "Save"

**Your skills are synced to:**
- Airtable talent pool
- Project matching system
- Client proposals

### Updating Banking Details

1. Navigate to your profile
2. Click "Banking Details"
3. Click "Edit"
4. Update your information
5. Click "Save"

**Security:**
- Banking details are encrypted
- Only authorized finance staff can view them
- Changes are audit logged

### Profile Sync

When you update your profile:
- Changes save immediately to the database
- Sync to Airtable happens within 30 seconds
- You'll see a confirmation message

## Updating Availability

### Availability Statuses

**Available**
- You're ready for new projects
- Can take on full-time work
- Visible in talent pool searches

**Partially Available**
- You have some capacity
- Can take on part-time work
- Specify your available percentage

**Unavailable**
- You're fully booked
- Not available for new projects
- Hidden from talent pool searches

### How to Update

1. Navigate to your profile
2. Find "Availability" section
3. Select your status
4. Add notes if needed (e.g., "Available from March 1")
5. Click "Update"

### Automatic Updates

Your availability may be automatically updated when:
- You're assigned to a new project
- Your utilization reaches certain thresholds
- Projects end

### Utilization Tracking

- System tracks your allocated hours
- Calculates utilization percentage
- Alerts sent if over-utilized

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How often should I update my profile?**
A: Update immediately when information changes, and review quarterly for accuracy.

**Q: Can I have multiple email addresses?**
A: No, each consultant has one primary email address for the portal.

**Q: How do I change my email address?**
A: Contact support to update your email address.

**Q: When will I get paid?**
A: Payments are processed monthly based on approved timesheets in Harvest.

**Q: How do I track my time?**
A: Time tracking is done in Harvest. You'll receive separate login details.

**Q: Can I see my payment history?**
A: Contact the finance team for payment history and statements.

**Q: What if I'm going on leave?**
A: Update your availability status to "Unavailable" and add notes about your return date.

**Q: How do I report a bug?**
A: Email support with details about the issue, including screenshots if possible.

## Glossary

**OTP**: One-Time Password - A temporary code sent to your email for login

**Airtable**: The system where Up Time Consulting manages consultant data

**Harvest**: Time tracking system for logging hours worked

**Xero**: Accounting system for processing payments

**ClickUp**: Project management tool for tasks and collaboration

**Utilization**: Percentage of your available time allocated to projects
